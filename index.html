Reading note — the small colored square in each room's corner is the marker color from the original sketch — it's not a room-type code (green marks four separate bedrooms), only a tag to keep that source detail on the page; the table below lists it separately from what the room actually is. Walls are drawn as one continuous unbroken line with real door openings cut into them (swing arcs where the sketch showed one, plain gaps at the kitchen's open pass-through and the lift's sliding door) and window breaks on the exterior walls. Dimensions are copied as written (apostrophe used as decimal point, e.g. "3'4m" → 3.4m). Four shapes are genuinely irregular, not boxes: Room A's soffit-cut corner, the stepped entry foyer, the offset stairwell shaft, and the L-shaped Salón with its sofa alcove.
